Safety Guidelines for Milk Handling

  • ✅ Always wash hands before expressing or handling milk.
  • ✅ Use sterilized bottles and containers only.
  • ✅ Label milk with date and time of collection.
  • ✅ Store milk in a clean refrigerator or freezer.
  • ✅ Deliver milk in a temperature-controlled container.
