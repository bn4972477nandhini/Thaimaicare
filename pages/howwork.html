How to Use Mother’s Milk

Mother’s milk is the best and safest food for a baby. Always wash your hands before handling the milk. Collect the milk in a clean bottle or storage bag and close it properly. Write the date and time, then store it in the fridge or freezer. When the baby needs milk, warm the bottle by placing it in warm water—do not boil. Feed the baby using a clean bottle or cup. If any milk is left after feeding, throw it away and do not use it again.
